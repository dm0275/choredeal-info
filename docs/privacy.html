ChoreDeal information

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

ChoreDeal is provided by Daniel Mancilla. This policy explains how information is handled when you use ChoreDeal or contact support. For privacy questions, contact support@fussionlabs.com.

Information used by the app

ChoreDeal helps families manage chores, agreements, reviews and rewards. It stores the information you enter, including household and member names, roles, chore descriptions, schedules, completion notes, review feedback, reward amounts, earnings, recorded payments and activity history. It also uses identifiers to associate members with their household and Apple iCloud identity and to synchronize changes reliably.

Reward and payment records are bookkeeping only. ChoreDeal does not transfer money or ask for bank account or payment card details. Apple handles purchasing the app through the App Store.

The app keeps a local copy of household information and pending changes on your iPhone. Device settings, notification context and read status help the app work offline and display updates. When you connect a household to iCloud, ChoreDeal uses Apple's CloudKit services to store and synchronize household information with invited family members. ChoreDeal does not operate a separate developer-hosted server for household data or require a separate ChoreDeal login.

Sharing within your household

Household information is shared through iCloud with invited participants. Invite only people you trust. Parent and teenager views organize what the app displays and allows, but they do not create separate private cloud records for each teenager. Participants with write access can potentially access the full shared household data through another client.

Removing an app membership alone is not the same as revoking iCloud sharing access. The household owner can use Household → Invite & manage sharing to remove a participant’s iCloud access. Other participants may retain information they have already viewed or copied, even after access is revoked.

Notifications

ChoreDeal uses Apple's notification services. Notifications may include member names, chore details, earned rewards or recorded payment amounts. These details can appear on your Lock Screen depending on your iPhone settings. You can control notification permissions and previews in iPhone Settings.

Support messages

If you email support, Daniel Mancilla receives your email address, your message and any attachments or screenshots you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request and investigate the problem. Support email is handled through Google Workspace.

Resolved support emails and screenshots are deleted within 90 days of resolution. Please share only what is needed to explain the issue, remove unrelated personal details from screenshots, and do not send passwords or a full household export. You may contact support to request earlier deletion of information you have sent.

Crash reports and analytics

Daniel Mancilla reviews crash reports and aggregated analytics made available by Apple to find problems and improve ChoreDeal. These reports can describe technical failures, device or operating-system characteristics, and overall app usage or App Store performance. Apple's sharing settings govern the diagnostic and app-usage information it provides; App Store sales and download reporting is separate.

ChoreDeal does not include a third-party analytics or advertising SDK or its own analytics-upload service. Reports and analytics are viewed only in Apple’s tools; Daniel Mancilla does not download and retain separate copies. Apple manages the availability and retention of reports in its services. The 90-day support retention period above applies to resolved support correspondence and screenshots; it does not set Apple's report-retention period.

For more information, see Apple's privacy policy and Apple's explanation of app analytics.

Advertising and tracking

ChoreDeal has no ads and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising. Household information is not sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Keeping and removing household information

Household information remains on participating devices and, when connected, in iCloud until removed through the relevant controls. In Household → Manage household data:

  • Delete household: The original owner can delete the shared household from iCloud and remove the local copy. This also removes the household's earnings and payment records; it does not settle any money owed outside the app.
  • Leave household: An invited member can leave and revoke their access through the departure flow. Shared history, including their earlier activity, remains with the household.
  • Remove from this iPhone: Removes this device's local household copy. It does not delete the cloud household or end participation, and an existing cloud household may be restored later.

Deleting an individual chore removes its associated chore history but preserves earned money and recorded payments as standalone reward and payment records. Minimal identifiers remain to prevent deleted chores from reappearing during synchronization. Uninstalling the app does not delete the iCloud household.

Cloud removal needs a working connection and the appropriate iCloud account. If removal cannot finish, follow the app's retry guidance. Offline changes can remain on a device until uploaded or discarded. Deletion does not guarantee immediate erasure from other devices, previously delivered notifications, screenshots, exported copies or backups. ChoreDeal does not currently provide a separate action to erase all of one member's information from retained shared history.

Contact support for help understanding these options. Support does not have a separate administrative service for opening or deleting your private household records.

Family use

ChoreDeal is designed for use within a trusted family, including parents and teenagers. Parents should help younger family members understand what is shared and avoid including sensitive personal information in chore descriptions, notes or support messages. The app does not request access to contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Services outside ChoreDeal

Apple operates iCloud, notifications and the App Store. Google Workspace processes support email. Their services have their own privacy terms. See Apple's privacy policy and Google Workspace's privacy information.

If you read this policy or support information on GitHub or GitHub Pages, GitHub also processes website-visitor information. GitHub states that Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when ChoreDeal's practices change, with the revised date shown at the top. For questions or requests concerning information you have provided, contact Daniel Mancilla at support@fussionlabs.com.
