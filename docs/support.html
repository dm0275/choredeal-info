Support

Draft for review — not yet published.

For help with ChoreDeal, email support@fussionlabs.com. ChoreDeal is provided by Daniel Mancilla.

Please describe what you were doing, what happened, your app version and your iPhone's iOS version. A screenshot can help, but remove unrelated personal information first. Do not send passwords, payment card details or a full household export. Resolved support emails and screenshots are deleted within 90 days of resolution.

Household updates

Check Household → Sync status for pending changes or an issue that needs attention. Confirm that your phone has a working connection and uses the Apple Account associated with the household. Keep the app updated on every family device.

Privacy and data removal

See the privacy policy for storage, sharing and removal details. Household → Manage household data offers different actions for deleting a household, leaving it or removing its local copy. Leaving preserves shared family history; removing the local copy does not delete the iCloud household. Avoid uninstalling the app as a way to delete cloud data.

Rewards and payments

ChoreDeal records earnings and payments your family handles outside the app. It does not transfer money. For App Store billing or refund requests, use Apple's purchase support.
